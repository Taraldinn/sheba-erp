# Operations, Staff & Reseller Pages

`IMPLEMENTED`

This section details the operational support, inventory logistics, workforce management, and reseller hierarchy screens implemented in the frontend.

---

## 1. Helpdesk & Support Tickets (`/support`)

### Purpose
Centralized ticketing system for customer technical complaints, fiber issues, billing disputes, and service restoration tracking.

### Route & Permission
- **Route**: `/support`
- **Role Guard**: `super_admin`, `admin`, `support`, `technician`

### Components Breakdown
```text
SupportPage
 ├── TicketKPIs (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Escalated)
 ├── TicketFilters (Priority: Low, Normal, High, Urgent; Status; Category)
 ├── TicketsTable
 │    ├── TicketID, Customer Name, Category, Priority Badge, Assigned Staff, Status
 │    └── Actions (View Details, Assign Technician, Update Status)
 ├── CreateTicketDialog (Customer Search, Subject, Issue Category, Initial Comment)
 └── TicketDetailDrawer (Timeline of comments, technician notes, resolution timestamp)
```

## 2. Field Tasks & Dispatch (`/tasks`)

### Purpose
Assigns, tracks, and verifies on-site tasks for field technicians (e.g., optical fiber splicing, new customer home installations, drop-cable replacements).

### Route & Permission
- **Route**: `/tasks`
- **Role Guard**: `super_admin`, `admin`, `technician`

### Key Features
- **Task Board / List**: Displays task title, priority, customer location/zone, assigned technician, and scheduled completion time.
- **Status Progression**: `Pending` -> `In Transit` -> `On Site` -> `Completed` -> `Verified`.

---

## 3. Hardware Inventory (`/inventory`)

### Purpose
Tracks warehouse stock of physical networking equipment, optical accessories, and customer premises equipment (CPE) with serial tracking.

### Route & Permission
- **Route**: `/inventory`
- **Role Guard**: `super_admin`, `admin`, `technician`

### Tracked Items:
- **Routers**: MikroTik hEX, hAP ac2/ac3, CCR models.
- **ONUs**: XPON / GPON single-port and dual-band Wi-Fi optical terminals.
- **Optical Accessories**: Patch cords, fiber splitters (1:8, 1:16), TJ boxes, fast connectors.
- **Actions**: Add item, assign serial to customer, mark defective/RMA, low-stock threshold alerts.

---

## 4. Reports & Business Analytics (`/reports`)

### Purpose
Generates executive, financial, and operational reports with export capabilities (CSV / Excel).

### Route & Permission
- **Route**: `/reports`
- **Role Guard**: `super_admin`, `admin`, `billing`

### Available Reports:
- **Revenue & Collections**: Cash vs. digital collection breakdown by payment gateway and collector.
- **Due & Aging Report**: Customers overdue for 30, 60, or 90+ days.
- **Subscriber Churn & Growth**: New activations vs. disconnected accounts over time.
- **Bandwidth Consumption Summary**: Average and peak usage per package tier.

---

## 5. Resellers & Sub-ISPs (`/resellers`)

### Purpose
Manages multi-tier reseller partnerships (L1 Resellers and L2 Sub-dealers), wholesale package pricing, commission structures, and credit wallet balances.

### Route & Permission
- **Route**: `/resellers`
- **Role Guard**: `super_admin`, `admin`, `reseller`

### Components & Actions:
- **Reseller Cards & Table**: Reseller code, name, tier (L1/L2), active subscriber count, wallet balance.
- **Add Balance Dialog**: Credits funds to reseller's balance for automated subscriber recharges.
- **Commission Ledger**: Real-time commission earnings per subscriber package renewal.

---

## 6. Staff & Human Resources (`/staff`, `/hr`)

- **`/staff`**: Internal directory of staff accounts, department assignments, and system permission roles (`admin`, `billing`, `support`, `technician`).
- **`/hr`**: Employee profile management, monthly attendance logging, basic salary settings, and salary disbursement tracking.

---

## 7. Call Center CRM (`/callcenter`)

### Purpose
Logs inbound and outbound customer support calls, agent call handling durations, caller caller-ID identification, and quick-access CRM ticket creation.

---

## 8. Customer Wallets & Offers (`/wallet`, `/offers`)

- **`/wallet`**: Customer prepaid balance ledger, showing top-up transactions, automatic monthly subscription debits, and balance adjustments.
- **`/offers`**: Promotional discount campaigns, seasonal offers, and coupon code configurations for customer acquisition.
